Who we are

About Blend Vocal

A vibrant treble choir focused on connection, growth, and great music.

Blend Vocal Association of Calgary brings together talented singers who share a passion for vocal excellence. We come from all walks of life, united by song and a commitment to creating something beautiful as a collective.

🎵

What we sing

A wide variety of music, from folk and jazz to contemporary, rock and pop, with fresh arrangements that challenge us and delight audiences.

📅

When we rehearse

Thursdays, 7:00–9:15 PM in Southwest Calgary, starting September 3. Plus two weekend workshops each season to polish our sound.

🤝

How it feels

A welcoming, supportive space where every voice matters and every singer is invested in our shared sound.

New this season

The Ensemble

A small ensemble drawn from the wider treble choir: singers who consistently demonstrate high-calibre musicianship, reliability, and a commitment to Blend's vision. The Ensemble takes on expanded performance opportunities, including future touring, and helps lead our work in visibility, fundraising capacity, partnerships, and collaborations throughout the season.

Our music director

Réjean Campbell

Réjean Campbell is a conductor, educator, singer, and music director from Calgary, Alberta. With nearly two decades of musical performance and direction to his credit, Réjean focuses his leadership on building relationships within his groups to foster a culture of trust, growth and collaboration.

His work has earned him a Calgary Theatre Critic's Award nomination (Fiddler on the Roof, Painted Fish Performing Arts Society) and multiple Calgary-ACTS CAT Award nominations. Réjean is Artistic Director of Blend Vocal and the Big Rock Singers, and holds ongoing educator positions at Storybook Theatre's Theatre School, Morpheus Theatre's G&S Junior Theatre School, Chinook School of Music, and Mount Royal Conservatory.

He is a long-standing member of the Calgary Opera Chorus and performs regularly with several professional and semi-professional choirs and orchestras. His conducting mentors include Dr. Timothy Shantz (Artistic Director of the Juno Award-nominated Luminous Voices), Kenneth Kiesler (University of Michigan), and Dr. Mark Sirett (Member of the Order of Canada). When not making music, Réjean loves to ski, hike, and rock climb in the Canadian Rockies.
