Sing with us

Looking for a new choral challenge? Blend Vocal is a close-knit group of experienced treble singers, holding auditions for each season under the direction of Music Director Réjean Campbell.

Auditions: Thursday, September 3, 2026, 6:00–7:00 PM.

Ready to audition? Fill out our audition form to book an audition, and we'll be in touch to set up a time. Questions first? Email blendvocal@gmail.com.

What we're looking for

Vocal ability

Be prepared to sing a solo piece that shows off your range and style. We love jazz, musical theatre, and pop, so a song from one of these genres is perfect. Please bring an accompaniment track.

Harmony skills

We'll check your ability to hold your own in a group and blend with other voices.

Sight-singing

You'll be asked to demonstrate reading music and sight-singing. A bit rusty? That's totally okay. A willingness to try and a commitment to learn your part is all we ask.

The commitment

  • Rehearsals: Thursdays, 7:00–9:15 PM in Southwest Calgary, starting September 3.
  • Performances: A full program each fall and spring season, learned and memorized.
  • Workshops: Two six-hour weekend workshops per season to polish our sound and have fun together.
  • Volunteering: Every member joins a committee (fundraising, concert planning or another area) to help the choir thrive.

A path to the Ensemble

Auditions for the treble choir take place this fall. Singers who join and show the musical strength and collaborative mindset needed for small-ensemble work may be invited to take part in Ensemble projects, with expanded performance opportunities, including future touring. Learn more about the Ensemble.

Other ways to get involved

We're also seeking sponsorship and performance opportunities. If you like what you see and think you can help, please reach out. We'd love to hear from you.
