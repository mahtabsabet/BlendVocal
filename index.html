Who we are

Great music, made together

Blend Vocal Association of Calgary is a vibrant auditioned treble choir focused on connection, growth, and great music. We come from all walks of life, united by a love of singing and a commitment to making something beautiful together.

We rehearse weekly in a supportive space where every voice matters — a good fit for experienced singers looking for a group that's dedicated to the craft and to growing together.
